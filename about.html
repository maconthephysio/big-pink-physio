My story

Hi, I'm Mason

I'm Mason (Mace), an APA Titled Sports and Exercise Physiotherapist and Women's and Men's Pelvic Health Physiotherapist (and also a Practitioner in Hand Therapy and Strength and Conditioning Coach, just to round things out).

Since graduating in 2015, I have worked across both sports and general musculoskeletal caseloads alongside pelvic health, and since 2020 I have focused increasingly on pelvic pain and sexual dysfunction in both men and women. On the sports side, I have spent years working with rugby league players at local, regional, and state levels, as well as powerlifters and strongman athletes at national and international levels.

I love working with people from all walks of life, but I have a particular passion for supporting the LGBTQIA+ community; athletes of every level, from weekend warriors to high performers, particularly those dealing with hip or pelvic pain or returning to sport after pregnancy or surgery; and people with pelvic pain or sexual dysfunction who have been told by GPs or specialists that they have to "wait it out."

My approach starts with understanding what actually matters to you: what is being impacted, what you are working toward, and what makes you tick. My job is to help you get back to the things that matter, not just make and manage a diagnosis.

I have completed further training in prostatectomy rehabilitation, male pelvic floor and pelvic pain management, post-pregnancy rehabilitation, and pelvic pain in athletes, and I'm currently working toward additional Titling as a Women's, Men's, and Pelvic Health (WMPH) Physiotherapist, to be dual titled alongside Sports and Exercise.

Pelvic pain and sexual dysfunction are still hugely under-recognised and under-referred, and delayed diagnosis can take a real toll on quality of life, so my goal is for every patient to feel they can speak openly and confidently about what they are experiencing, and to have full confidence in the plan we build together toward their goals.

Outside the clinic, you will usually find me at the gym or walking my Bull Terrier, Shark, along the Northern Beaches.

How I work

What you can count on

Express it how you feel it

Pelvic floor dysfunction, overactive bladder, anorectal pain: they are the technical terms, but if you experience pain with urinating, orgasm, bottoming / pegging, or generally feel not quite right downstairs, then that is what it means to you.

Physio for everybody and every body

Pelvic floor issues can affect everyone, and this kind of physiotherapy is for everyone from all stages of life. My goal is to eliminate the barriers in seeking equitable healthcare for those with these issues who need it.

It works, and we care

Everything we do is guided by the latest and most robust literature available: practical, active, easy to digest and integrate into day-to-day life, all for your benefit, and nothing too left of field.
